## 4. Safety & Quality Standards

### Headline: Safety and Quality in Every Project.
- **Subheadline:** Clear practices, careful execution, and adherence to project standards.
- **Lead:** In commercial electrical contracting, safety and quality are fundamental to successful delivery. AStar Electricals maintains disciplined practices on every project site, working in accordance with approved procedures, site safety rules, and relevant regulations.

### Safety Practices: On-Site Safety as a Priority
- **Site Safety Adherence:** We follow established site safety protocols, wear required personal protective equipment (PPE), and respect site-specific rules on every project.
- **Pre-Work Briefings:** Work teams review daily tasks, identify potential hazards, and confirm safety measures before commencing work.
- **Safe Working Procedures:** Electrical tasks are carried out following established safe work methods, including isolation procedures where applicable.
- **Housekeeping and Order:** Work areas are kept organised to reduce slip, trip, and hazard risks during and after work shifts.

### Quality Approach: Consistent Execution
- **Work to Specifications:** All installations are completed in accordance with approved project drawings, technical documentation, and product guidelines.
- **Material Verification:** Materials and components used on site are checked against approved submittals before installation.
- **In-Progress Checks:** Work is inspected at key stages to confirm quality and compliance before the next phase begins.
- **Defect Rectification:** Any identified issues are addressed promptly to maintain project momentum and standards.

### Standards Alignment: Working Within Frameworks
- We work in alignment with applicable Indian Standards (IS), the National Electrical Code (NEC), local electricity board requirements, and client-specified project standards.
